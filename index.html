QUESTIONS & ANSWERS STAKEHOLDER CLARITY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Find clear answers tailored for enterprise leaders, sponsors, alumni mentors, and prospective Generation 2 candidates.

Enterprises can submit capstone proposals by contacting the TIM Program Office or filling out our engagement form below. Proposals are evaluated by our joint USTH-French academic committee, and matched with Gen 2 Master candidates for a 9-month execution phase starting in September.
We offer three sponsorship tiers: Fellowship Sponsor (€5,000/student), Innovation Lab Sponsor (€25,000/year for equipment & co-branding), and Chair Sponsor (€50,000+ for multi-year co-R&D initiatives with full IP co-ownership options).
Gen 2 applicants require a Bachelor degree in STEM, Engineering, Business, or Economics with demonstrated interest in technology management. The 2-year curriculum covers Technology Strategy, Intellectual Property & Patent Commercialization, Data-Driven Innovation, and a mandatory 6 to 9-month Master thesis/capstone.
Gen 1 alumni can join the TIM Alumni Advisory Circle by submitting the "Pay It Forward" contact form. Alumni mentors receive invitations to guest lecture, review capstone midterms, and sit on final defense panels alongside French professors.
