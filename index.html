WAYS TO WORK WITH US.

Four ways to engage with the Master in Technology and Innovation Management at USTH, each with a clear contribution and a clear return.

FRESH EYES ON HARD PROBLEMS.

Bring us what your organisation is working through: an operational bottleneck, a strategic question, or a research topic. A cross-sector team of managers and specialists works on it with your data, under faculty guidance, and delivers practical solutions or research findings your team can use.

You bring: a challenge or research question, data you can share, and one contact person.

You gain: solutions and insight from multiple industries, backed by academic rigour, at no cost.

TALENT AND VISIBILITY.

Meet managers and specialists already working in technology and innovation, and position your organisation alongside innovation education.

You gain: access to a talent pool and brand visibility.

EXPERT EXCHANGE.

Join a short session after class or a guest talk with managers from many industries.

You gain: new perspectives, collaboration opportunities and a stronger professional profile.

STARTUP MENTORING.

Teams of three students from different sectors mentor a student startup, one session per month for a semester.

You gain: experienced mentors at no cost.

RESEARCH COLLABORATION.

Faculty projects gain participants with industry experience, real cases and enterprise data.

You gain: stronger links between research and practice.

COMMERCIALISATION SUPPORT.

Students and their enterprise networks help explore the commercialisation and IP valuation of research results.

Status: exploratory, developed case by case.

RETURN AS A SPEAKER OR MENTOR.

Share your experience with current cohorts through talks or startup mentoring.

HIRE FROM THE NETWORK.

Find candidates among current students and fellow alumni across industries.

BRING YOUR ORGANISATION'S CHALLENGE.

Turn a challenge at your workplace into a project for the next cohort.

START WITH A NETWORK.

Inherit active partners, ongoing projects and contacts from the cohorts before you.

LEARN FROM THOSE BEFORE YOU.

A handbook of practical lessons on working with each partner, written by previous cohorts.

TEST YOUR OWN IDEAS.

Use the program as a testing ground for challenges, products and services from your own organisation, with feedback from faculty and experts.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS STAKEHOLDER CLARITY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Find clear answers tailored for enterprise leaders, sponsors, alumni mentors, and prospective Generation 2 candidates.

Enterprises can submit capstone proposals by contacting the TIM Program Office or filling out our engagement form below. Proposals are evaluated by our joint USTH-French academic committee, and matched with Gen 2 Master candidates for a 9-month execution phase starting in September.
We offer three sponsorship tiers: Fellowship Sponsor (€5,000/student), Innovation Lab Sponsor (€25,000/year for equipment & co-branding), and Chair Sponsor (€50,000+ for multi-year co-R&D initiatives with full IP co-ownership options).
Gen 2 applicants require a Bachelor degree in STEM, Engineering, Business, or Economics with demonstrated interest in technology management. The 2-year curriculum covers Technology Strategy, Intellectual Property & Patent Commercialization, Data-Driven Innovation, and a mandatory 6 to 9-month Master thesis/capstone.
Gen 1 alumni can join the TIM Alumni Advisory Circle by submitting the "Pay It Forward" contact form. Alumni mentors receive invitations to guest lecture, review capstone midterms, and sit on final defense panels alongside French professors.
